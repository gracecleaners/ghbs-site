Who We Are

Great Harvest Bankers SACCO

Our Direction

Vision, Mission & Values

Our strategic framework guides every decision we make and every product we offer, ensuring we stay true to our purpose.

Vision

A countrywide prosperous member-focused Savings, Credit and Investment Institution by 2028

Mission

Our Journey

Our Story So Far

Key milestones in the growth and development of Great Harvest Bankers SACCO.

2016
Established

Great Harvest Bankers SACCO was founded by a group of visionary Bank of Uganda staff members committed to creating a culture of savings and investment.

2021
Official Registration

GHBS was officially registered on 26th October 2021 under the Ministry of Trade, Industry and Cooperatives, gaining full legal status as a SACCO.

2023
Product Expansion

Expanded product portfolio to include multiple savings accounts, loan products, and comprehensive insurance coverage for members.

2025
Digital Transformation

Embracing technology to automate processes, enhance member experience, and position GHBS for sustainable growth into the future.

Strategic Focus

Strategic Goals

Four key pillars that define our strategic direction and guide our growth agenda.

01
A Viable SACCO
  1. Grow a motivated membership base through offering attractive products and services
  2. Invest in risk managed opportunities for high and medium returns
  3. Leverage SACCO assets with debt and undertake profitable joint ventures
  4. Build and support members' capacity to develop viable micro-enterprises
  5. Broaden membership eligibility criteria to include extended family members
02
Robust Institutional Capacity
  1. Build and strengthen SACCO governance
  2. Competent management skills
  3. Institute and operate efficient internal processes
03
A Technology Enabled SACCO
  1. Invest in digital systems to automate business processes
  2. Preserve SACCO and customer assets with effective cyber security technologies
  3. Promote appropriate technologies in member business
04
Value Generated Partnerships
  1. Enter into strategic alliances with prospective organizations
  2. Explore and exploit available institutional development and member focused grant opportunities
  3. Develop a market for onward lending opportunities amongst members
