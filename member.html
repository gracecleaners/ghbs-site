Requirements

Membership Requirements

To become a member of the SACCO, applicants are required to meet the following requirements:

Minimum Savings

UGX 1,000,000

Share Capital

200 Shares at UGX 15,000 per share

Total Share Capital Requirement

UGX 3,000,000

(200 shares × UGX 15,000)
Annual Subscription Fee

UGX 50,000

Membership Registration Fee

UGX 50,000

Total Initial Contribution: Minimum savings (UGX 1,000,000) + Share Capital (UGX 3,000,000) + Registration Fee (UGX 50,000) = UGX 4,050,000 (plus annual subscription of UGX 50,000)
